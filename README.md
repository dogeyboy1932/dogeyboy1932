### Beyond Code

**Uploaded intelligence.** I got pulled deep into the sci-fi series *Pantheon* and have not really climbed out. The premise is mapping a human brain and running it as a digital simulation, but the show refuses to treat that as a gimmick. If a digital consciousness perceives itself as alive, feels emotion, and reacts exactly as the biological original would, is its reality any less valid than ours? We anchor our definition of real in the physical world, but our entire physical experience is already just a brain interpreting signals. If the signals are simulated and perceived identically, the boundary collapses. I keep landing on the same conclusion: existence is better defined by the act of perceiving than by the medium, flesh or code. Same reason *Blade Runner* and *Mr. Robot* stuck with me.

**Chess and tennis.** Both are pattern recognition, planning, and the patience to hold a line. It is the closest thing I have to a description of how I approach engineering.

**Eagle Scout.** Long projects, other people depending on you finishing them.

**Singapore.** Studied at NUS in fall 2024. First real exposure to the wider world and a genuine turning point in how I think about what to build and who for.
